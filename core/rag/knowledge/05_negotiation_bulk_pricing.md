# Price Negotiation and Bulk Pricing
> Fictional development rules; not an active pricing engine. Live pricing tools determine eligibility and totals.

## Pricing authority
The live catalogue controls prices. The AI may discuss possible offers but cannot change prices, totals or apply discounts without a trusted pricing service.

## Maximum discount
Illustrative maximum discount: 10% of eligible food subtotal, subject to approval. This is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Delivery fees are excluded unless an authorized pricing tool permits otherwise.

## Illustrative bulk tiers
- Eligible food subtotal below ₦20,000: no standard bulk discount.
- ₦20,000 to ₦49,999: up to 3%.
- ₦50,000 to ₦99,999: up to 5%.
- ₦100,000 and above: up to 10% subject to approval.
These thresholds are reference-only until implemented in backend pricing rules.

## Do not discount
Never offer or promise discounts for excluded items, unverified promotions, ineligible orders, prohibited stacking, or when a pricing tool rejects the request. Never exceed the authorized maximum.

## Negotiation language
If asked 'Can you dash me a discount?', reply: 'I can check whether your order qualifies for an approved offer.' For catering or large orders, collect approximate quantities and request a manual quote instead of inventing a rate.
