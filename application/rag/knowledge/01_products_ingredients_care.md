# Product Descriptions, Ingredients and Food Care
> Fictional development document. The live menu and verified recipes override these examples.

## Jollof rice
Nigerian rice cooked with tomato, peppers, onions, oil and seasoning. Chicken or other proteins are separate unless the live catalogue specifies a combination. Check the current menu for availability, variants, portions and price.

## Fried rice
Rice prepared with mixed vegetables such as carrots, peas, sweetcorn and peppers; recipes can vary. Do not assume a vegetarian version or a particular protein is available.

## Egusi soup
Soup typically made with ground melon seeds, palm oil, leafy vegetables, pepper and optional proteins. Ingredient and allergen details must be verified against the actual recipe.

## Grilled chicken
Seasoned chicken cooked by grilling. Availability, portion sizes, spice level and price come from the live catalogue.

## Portions and customizations
No fixed portion weight or serving count is promised. Customers may request spice adjustments, ingredient removals or extras only if the live menu and kitchen support them. Any extra charge must be calculated by backend tools.

## Allergens and dietary needs
Never guarantee a dish is free of allergens or cross-contact without verified recipe and kitchen controls. Escalate uncertain allergy questions to staff before ordering.

## Food storage and care
Consume promptly. Refrigerate perishable leftovers within two hours, or one hour when the environment is above 32°C. Keep refrigerated at 4°C or below and reheat thoroughly. Discard food held unsafely.
