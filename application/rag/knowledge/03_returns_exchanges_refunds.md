# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds
> Fictional development policy. Applicable consumer rights take precedence.

## Food returns
Prepared food generally cannot be returned for resale or exchanged merely because the customer changed their mind. This does not remove statutory rights for faulty, unsafe or incorrectly supplied goods.

## Eligible problems
Customers may request review for wrong or missing items, unacceptable food condition, or verified payment for an unfulfilled order.

## Reporting window
Customers are encouraged to report a problem within 24 hours of delivery with an order reference, explanation and photographs where appropriate. Later complaints may still require review under law or exceptional circumstances.

## Remedies
After review, authorized staff may approve replacement, partial refund, full refund or another lawful remedy. The AI must never guarantee an outcome or issue a refund on its own.

## Payment and processing
Verify that the original payment succeeded before a refund. An order marked pending_payment is not proof of payment. Approved refund processing depends on the supported payment provider.
