# Delivery Policy
> Fictional development policy. The delivery-zone database and operational service are authoritative.

## Areas
Example Lagos neighbourhoods include Ikeja, Maryland, Ogba, Yaba and Surulere. These are illustrative, not guaranteed service zones. Check the customer's delivery area against the live database before promising delivery.

## Fees
Fees are determined by the live delivery-zone service at checkout. Do not invent a flat fee, waiver or distance-based calculation.

## Estimated times
An illustrative delivery estimate is 45–90 minutes after order acceptance, subject to traffic, weather, distance and kitchen load. The live service may provide a different estimate. Never guarantee arrival within this range.

## Hours and cut-off
Fictional order hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM West Africa Time. Fictional same-day delivery cut-off: 8:00 PM West Africa Time. Live operational status takes priority; after cut-off, do not promise same-day delivery.

## Delays and wrong addresses
For a delay, request the order reference and check authorized status tools or refer to support. Address changes require business approval and may be impossible after dispatch. Do not promise refunds or delivery changes without confirmation.
